Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the ResolveDesk project handles information when its customer-support and complaint-management features are used.

Effective date: October 20, 2026
Before publishing: This is a starter policy for a student project. Review it and replace any statements that do not match your actual implementation, hosting, retention practices, or contact details. Do not submit it to Meta until it accurately describes your service.

1. About ResolveDesk

ResolveDesk is a developing software project intended to help teams organize customer messages and complaints, assign cases to team members, and track status and resolution. Features and data handling may change while the project is under development.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on enabled features and configuration, ResolveDesk may process:

  • Contact details and messaging identifiers supplied by a messaging platform, such as a WhatsApp sender identifier and profile name.
  • Message content, timestamps, message identifiers, delivery/status events, and related conversation history.
  • Complaint records, categories, priority, case status, assignments, and action history created by workspace users.
  • Account and workspace information needed for authentication, authorization, and administration.
  • Technical logs needed to troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and maintain operation.

Do not send payment-card data, passwords, government identity documents, or other highly sensitive information through the service unless a feature expressly supports it and appropriate safeguards are in place.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to receive and display customer messages, create and manage complaint cases, categorize and prioritize cases, assign work to authorized team members, maintain conversation and action history, provide workspace metrics, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service from misuse.

4. WhatsApp and other service providers

If WhatsApp integration is enabled, messages and related information are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp services and may be processed by Meta under its own terms and privacy policies. ResolveDesk may also rely on hosting, database, authentication, and monitoring providers to operate its features. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and the project's configuration.

ResolveDesk does not control third-party providers' privacy practices. Review the relevant provider policies before using the integration with real customer information.

5. Access and security

The project is intended to restrict workspace information to authorized users according to their roles. Reasonable safeguards should be used, such as access controls, protected credentials, and server-side permission checks. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

During development, test data may be used. Do not assume that a development deployment is suitable for confidential or production customer information.

6. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be available to authorized members of the relevant workspace and to service providers needed to operate enabled features. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or others. ResolveDesk does not intend to sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, to operate the service, or to meet applicable legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the project's implementation and configuration.

9. International processing

Depending on selected hosting and service providers, information may be processed in countries other than the country where it was collected. Applicable safeguards and requirements should be assessed before using the service with real customer information.
